The Marsh at Quill's Hollow

Chapter One

The marsh had a memory, Mireth knew, longer than any living thing in Quill's Hollow cared to admit. She had grown up on its edge — close enough that the smell of peat and black water lived in the walls of her mother's house, close enough that the lantern in the window each night was not decoration but defense.

— III —

Mireth"You're not from Quill's Hollow," she said — not a question.

The Stranger"No," he said, and the word came out like something he had to carry.

— IV —

She did not invite him in. She did not turn him away. She set the net down, opened the door the rest of the way, and stepped back — and that, in the marsh, was as much an invitation as anyone ever gave. He crossed the lamplight border like a man walking into deep water, and the door closed behind him, and the reeds outside stayed perfectly, impossibly still.

Mireth(whispering) "Hold still. This will sting."
