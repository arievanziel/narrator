Narrator — GUI v3 Control Variants

8 variants exploring different approaches to unobtrusive UI controls for opening/closing panels and bars. All share the same Ink aesthetic, story content, and functionality — only the control UI differs.

Standard Variants (5)

v1 — Bookmark Tabs
Side panel toggles as little vertical bookmark/pull-tabs attached to panel edges. Top/bottom bars use horizontal bookmark-style tabs that stay visible as use-cues.
Tabs stick out from edges like bookmarks in a book. Subtle, tactile, always visible.
tabsbook-likealways-visible
v2 — Top Bar Command Center
All controls live in a thin always-visible top bar. Side panel toggles, bottom bar toggle, audio toggle — everything in one place. No floating buttons.
Single command strip. Clean, centralized, nothing floating.
centralizedno-floatingcompact
v3 — Edge Rails
Thin vertical rails on screen edges for side panels. Thin horizontal rails for top/bottom bars. Click rails to toggle. Tiny dots at rail centers as use-cues.
Pure geometry. Lines and dots, no buttons or tabs. Most architectural.
geometricrailsminimal
v4 — Audio Hub
Audio bar is the central hub. When expanded, panel/bar toggles live on its right side. When collapsed, tiny dots on the line provide access. Everything from the audio bar.
Audio-first design. Controls integrated with the most-used element.
audio-firstintegratedhub
v5 — Ultra Minimal
Everything collapses to 1px lines with 4px dots at endpoints. Click dots to toggle. Hover enlarges to 6px. No text, no icons, no buttons — just dots and lines.
Maximum minimalism. Barely there UI. For when you just want to read.
dotslinesextreme-minimal

Creative Variants (3) — Book-Inspired

v6 — Ribbon Bookmarks
Silk ribbon bookmarks hanging from the top edge, like in old leather-bound books. Each ribbon opens a different panel/bar. Subtle sway animation. Ribbons shorten when pulled.
Most book-like. Silk ribbons with fabric texture. Elegant, nostalgic.
ribbonssilkbook-likeanimated
v7 — Dog-Eared Pages
Foldable corners at the four corners of the reading area. Click to fold/unfold. Each corner controls a panel or bar. Subtle triangular folds that lift on hover.
Tactile, worn-book feel. Like naturally dog-eared pages. Very organic.
cornerstactileorganic
v8 — Margin Marks
Pencil annotation marks in the margins, like a previous reader's notes. Small pencil strokes that darken on hover. Marks change when panel/bar is open (checkmark, circled).
Most literary. Like reading a used book with annotations. Personal, hand-drawn feel.
pencilannotationsliteraryhand-drawn

Extra Variant

v9 — Circle Buttons
Round circle buttons everywhere: half-round tabs on screen edges for side panels, half-round tabs at center for top/bottom bars, small circle toggles in the top bar, and small circle toggles in the audio bar. All locations sync active state.
Circular motif throughout. Multiple access points for every control. Clear, tactile, consistent.
circleshalf-roundmulti-accessconsistent

What to look for: Each variant solves the same problem differently — how to provide unobtrusive but discoverable controls for opening/closing side panels, top bar, bottom bar, and audio bar. Try opening/closing everything in each variant. The best approach will feel natural and stay out of the way when you just want to read.

Common to all: Audio bar collapses to just a line with a breathing play button. Click the line to expand. Input area has generous bottom spacing. Both side panels can be open simultaneously. Top and bottom bars are full width.
