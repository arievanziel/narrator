The vaulted catacombs were a tomb of stone and stagnant water, each echoing drip a metronome of dread. The water, black as ink, rose inch by inch, swallowing the low arches and turning the floor into a shallow lake. Grimbold, a veteran whose armor bore the dents of countless battles, felt the chill seep into his boots. Beside him, Elara, a scholar clutching a trembling torch, tried to keep the flame alive as the darkness pressed in.

[SFX: _creeping_water_]

Grimbold "Keep your footing, Elara. The exit must be ahead—these walls have no end."

Elara "The water's rising faster than I thought. My notes are useless here."

A low, rhythmic sound began to pulse through the water, like a massive chest rising and falling. It was a breath, slow and deliberate, reverberating off the stone.

[SFX: _whispered_breath_]

Grimbold "Do you hear that? Something… breathing."

Elara "It’s… it’s coming from the depths. I can feel it watching."

The torch sputtered, casting frantic shadows that danced like specters. A sudden, sharp crack split the air as a slab of stone shifted, sending a spray of water into the gloom.

[SFX: _sudden_terror_]

Elara "What was that?!"

Grimbold "A collapse. Stay close!"

They pressed forward, water now up to their knees, the breathing growing louder, more urgent. From the blackness, a massive shape broke the surface—a hulking, serpentine form covered in algae, its eyes two cold, phosphorescent orbs. It lunged, water exploding outward.

[SFX: _surprise_splash_]

Grimbold raised his sword, but the creature’s maw opened, revealing rows of jagged teeth. Elara’s torch went out in an instant, plunging them into a suffocating blackness where only the creature’s breath and the rising water remained.
